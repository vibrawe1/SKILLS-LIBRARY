# Publication Gates

## Gate checklist

- Project type, scope, audience, language, and structure are approved.
- Canon is versioned; unresolved conflicts are explicitly accepted or blocking.
- Every chapter has a completed audit record.
- Global continuity, consistency, and quality audits are complete.
- Correction-cycle limits were respected.
- Final output was rendered or exported and independently checked where relevant.
- Rights, credits, sources, metadata, and platform requirements were checked for the intended destination.
- The user explicitly approved publication.

Any missing item is `BLOCKED` or `UNVERIFIED`, not `PUBLICATION_READY`. The skill may prepare files and a publication checklist, but it must not perform the publication action without distinct approval.
