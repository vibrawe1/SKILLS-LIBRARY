# Authority and Approvals Contract

## Authority order

1. User decision and explicit approval.
2. Approved project canon and recorded project constraints.
3. DIRECTOR editorial coordination within the approved scope.
4. WORKFLOW-MANAGER technical sequencing and state control.
5. Specialized role recommendations and transformations.
6. Unverified inference, model output, or external material.

Lower-level material cannot silently override higher-level authority.

## Required approval checkpoints

Ask for user approval before:

- fixing the project type when it is not already decided;
- promoting provisional material into canon;
- resolving a canon conflict by selecting one alternative;
- changing scope, audience, language, or publication target;
- accepting a third correction-cycle failure;
- making an external or irreversible change;
- publishing or submitting an artifact.

An agent may prepare a recommendation and an explicit decision record. It may not silently make the decision.

## Conflict handling

When two instructions conflict, record both, identify their source and authority, stop the affected path, and request a decision unless an already-approved precedence rule resolves the conflict. Do not hide the conflict by blending incompatible rules.
